Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How do I implement BrickMMO tracking?

Simply include our tracker.js file and configure your asset ID. See the About page for detailed integration methods.

❓ Is the tracking GDPR compliant?

Yes! We only collect anonymous usage statistics and don't track personal information or cross-site behavior.

❓ Can I track multiple websites?

Absolutely! Each website gets a unique asset ID, allowing you to track multiple properties separately in one dashboard.

❓ What about performance impact?

Our tracker is lightweight and loads asynchronously, ensuring minimal impact on your website's performance.
