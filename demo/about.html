About BrickMMO Stats

Our Mission

BrickMMO Stats is a powerful, easy-to-use analytics platform designed to help website owners understand their audience and improve their online presence. Built with simplicity and effectiveness in mind, our platform provides real-time insights that matter.

What We Offer

  • Real-time page view tracking
  • Detailed browser and device analytics
  • User-friendly admin dashboard
  • Privacy-focused data collection
  • Easy integration with any website
  • Comprehensive reporting tools

Our Technology

Built using modern web technologies including PHP, MySQL, and JavaScript, BrickMMO Stats offers reliable performance and scalability. Our tracking system is lightweight and won't slow down your website.
